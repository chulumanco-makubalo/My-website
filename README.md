## 1. Project Overview
Sibahle Naturals is a company that sells natural skincare and wellness products. 
The goal of this website is to showcase products, share the brand story, and allow customers to make enquiries online.

## 2. Website Goals and Objectives
1.  **Brand Awareness**: Introduce Sibahle Naturals and its natural product range
2.  **Customer Engagement**: Provide easy contact and enquiry options
3.  **Professional Presence**: Create a clean, natural, and trustworthy online presence

## 3. Target Audience
Health-conscious individuals looking for natural skincare, body care, and wellness products in South Africa.

## 4. Proposed Website Features and Functionality
- **Homepage**: Welcome banner, company intro, featured products
- **About Us**: Company story, mission, and values of Sibahle Naturals
- **Products/Services**: Catalog of natural products with descriptions
- **Contact**: Physical address, phone, email, social media links
- **Enquiry**: Contact form for product questions and orders

## 5. Design and User Experience
**Colour Scheme**: Natural Greens `#2E7D32`, Soft Cream `#FFF8E1`, Earth Brown `#8D6E63`
**Typography**: Headings: Montserrat Bold. Body: Open Sans Regular
**Layout and Design**: Clean, image-focused, lots of white space. Header with logo + nav on all pages
**User Experience**: Simple navigation, mobile-friendly, consistent branding across all pages
