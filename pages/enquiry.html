Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sell online?
Not yet. Send an enquiry with the products you're interested in, and we'll reply with availability, pricing and how to collect or arrange delivery.

Our range is formulated for 4C and afro-textured hair, and also works well for people transitioning from relaxed hair.

Yes, several of our products are gentle enough for kids' hair. Tell us your child's hair type in the enquiry form and we'll advise you.
