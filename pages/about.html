About Us

Sibahle Naturals is a natural hair care brand based in Gqeberha, South Africa. We make oils, creams, wash and styling products from organic oils and herbs, formulated for people with 4C and afro-textured hair.

Our Story

Before Sibahle Naturals had a name, it had a problem. Sibahle struggled for years to find hair products that worked for her curl pattern – most shelves offered either chemical-heavy formulas or products too harsh for her hair to handle.

She started mixing her own oils and herbs, testing whether natural, organic ingredients could actually do the job. Her grandmother, who was living with alopecia, agreed to try the blends. They worked. Encouraged by that, and by her grandmother asking for more, Sibahle kept refining the formulas until they were ready to share beyond her own family.

It started with my grandmother. Watching it help her is the reason there's a Sibahle Naturals at all.

— Sibahle, Founder

In 2022, Sibahle Naturals opened its first small shop in Gqeberha, offering a full range of oils, creams, wash and styling products, all built from those original family formulas. Four years on, the brand has a loyal base of customers and is ready to reach people beyond Gqeberha.

Mission and Vision

Mission: To create natural hair products that really work for 4C or any afro-textured hair, made with organic oils and herbals that help people maintain their hair easily and love their crown.

Vision: To be one of South Africa's trusted natural hair care ranges, known for producing quality results.

Who We Serve

  • People already natural, looking for products that genuinely work on their curl pattern
  • People with natural hair looking for non-harsh, organic products that moisturise and support their curl pattern
  • People with relaxed hair who want to transition to afro hair
  • Parents buying for their kids' natural hair, looking for products that are gentle on the scalp
  • Anyone with afro-textured or curly hair in and around Gqeberha

What We Believe

  • Made honest: organic, transparent ingredients, with no vague "natural blend" hiding behind the label
  • Made to work: formulated and tested for 4C and afro-textured hair, including hair recovering from conditions like alopecia
  • Made for you: guidance for every hair journey, whether you're natural, transitioning, or shopping for your kids
